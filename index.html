How it works

One monthly contribution. Four things happen to it.

The mechanics are the same rotating circle your family already trusts — we've simply put the idle float to work between collection and payout.

1

Contribute

Each of the 10 members pays a fixed amount monthly into the group's dedicated account — the same as any committee.

2

One member is paid out

By rotation or auction/bid, one member receives that month's full pool. Everyone gets their turn once per 10-month cycle.

3

The rest is invested

Contributions not yet due for payout are deployed into a Shariah-screened equity basket, minus a liquidity buffer.

4

Gains are shared

At cycle end, investment growth is distributed pro-rata to how long each member's money was in the market.

Compliance framework

Halal by construction, not by afterthought.

Every rupee in the investment sleeve passes sector and financial screens before it's eligible — reviewed on an ongoing basis by an appointed Shariah advisor, with any incidental impure income purified through charitable donation, never distributed to members.

MI
Mufti Imran Saifee
Shariah Advisor · Reviews screening methodology quarterly

  • Sector exclusions

    No exposure to alcohol, gambling, conventional interest-based finance, pork-related products, adult entertainment, or weapons manufacturing.

  • Financial ratio screens

    Standard debt-to-market-cap, interest income-to-revenue, and receivables-to-assets tests, in line with AAOIFI-style methodology.

  • Zero interest, structurally

    The mandatory liquidity buffer is held in non-interest-bearing current accounts or Shariah-compliant cash equivalents — never conventional interest.

  • Purification

    Any incidental non-compliant income identified during screening is calculated and donated to charity — never retained or paid to members.

FAQ

Questions members ask before joining

Is this actually Shariah-compliant, or just "halal-branded"?+

The investment methodology is reviewed on an ongoing basis by an appointed Shariah advisor against sector and financial-ratio screens comparable to established Indian Shariah fund methodologies. The full screening criteria are published and any impure income is purified through donation, not distributed.

What happens if my payout month's investments have lost value?+

Your payout is your contributed principal — it is never reduced by market movements. A liquidity buffer covering the next 1–2 scheduled payouts is held in non-market-linked form at all times specifically to guarantee this.

Can I leave the circle before my cycle ends?+

Yes, under a pre-agreed exit formula: you receive back the principal you've contributed, without a share of unrealised gains, to keep the structure fair to members who stay the full cycle.

What if another member defaults after receiving their payout?+

Every member provides a guarantor or security before their payout turn — standard chit-fund practice — to protect the group's remaining pool.

Who actually holds and manages the invested money?+

Funds sit in a dedicated group account, separate from personal or organiser funds, with an independent bookkeeper maintaining auditable records and monthly statements sent to every member regardless of payout status.
